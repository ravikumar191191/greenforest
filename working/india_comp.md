## CRITICAL HONESTY / METHODOLOGY NOTES

1. **No separate PM-T track exists on levels.fyi or 6figr for India.** Amazon's India pages are all generic **"Product Manager"** (titles: Product Manager / Senior Product Manager / Principal Product Manager). There is **no India "Product Manager, Technical / PMT" data split** on levels.fyi. The PMT-vs-PM distinction surfaces **only in anecdotal Blind threads** — and most of those threads quote **US TC in USD**, not India. So for the requester's exact role (PM-T), India structured data is **thin-to-absent**; the levels.fyi/6figr figures below are **generic PM**, used as the closest available proxy and labeled as such.
2. **levels.fyi gives the base/stock/bonus split in USD on its India pages** (it appears to convert; the headline range is shown in INR). I report the USD split exactly as the source shows it, and the INR median/range exactly as shown. **I do NOT convert USD→INR or US→India anywhere.** Where the source itself shows both, I reproduce both.
3. **Sample sizes for levels.fyi India PM/level cells are not disclosed in the page snippets** and are widely understood to be **very small (often single-digit to low-double-digit submissions)** for India PM. Treat every levels.fyi India PM cell as **LOW confidence / thin data** unless noted.
4. **Vesting:** Amazon = 4-yr back-loaded (5/15/40/40). Microsoft = 5-yr (20%/yr) on-hire grants + annual refreshers. Google = 4-yr (front-loaded ~33/33/22/12 under recent schedules). levels.fyi "stock /yr" figures are **annualized**, already smoothing the vest curve.
5. **Target bonus %:** Not shown as a field on levels.fyi India pages; inferred from program structure + anecdotes — Amazon India has **no cash target bonus** (comp = base + RSU + Y1/Y2 sign-on; bonus≈0 confirms this in the data); Microsoft target bonus ~**0-15%** band level dependent; Google target bonus ~**15%**.
6. Official sources (levels.fyi, 6figr) are separated from anecdotal (Blind, Glassdoor, AmbitionBox, Grapevine, blogs) and labeled.

## SECTION 5 — DISAGREEMENTS / FLAGS

- **levels.fyi vs Glassdoor (Google PM):** levels.fyi L5 PM India median **₹11.98M TC** vs Glassdoor Bangalore PM avg **₹27.5L**. Glassdoor is base-skewed and excludes/understates RSU; the gap is expected. Trust levels.fyi for TC, Glassdoor only for base direction.
- **levels.fyi vs 6figr (Amazon L6):** levels.fyi Amazon L6 PM **₹7.33M (~₹73L)** vs 6figr "Amazon L6" **₹110L** — the 6figr figure is **all-function L6 (SDM/SDE heavy), not PM**, and must not be used as an Amazon PM/PMT benchmark.
- **PM vs PM-T:** No India data source splits PMT from PM. Anecdotes suggest PMT sits at the **top of the PM band** (L6 PMT >₹90L vs PM ~₹55-84L; L7 PMT ~₹1.5-1.6 Cr vs non-tech PM ~₹90L-1.4 Cr). These are **anecdotal Blind India** only.
- **US contamination:** Several high "L6 PMT $350k" Blind posts are **US**, not India — excluded from India rows.
- **Sample size:** Every levels.fyi India PM cell is thin (sample n not published; understood single/low-double-digit). 6figr Microsoft PM2 n=35, Amazon-L6 n=376 (but not PM). Glassdoor India n=230-324 (base-skewed).
