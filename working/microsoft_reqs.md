## 1. LEVELING LADDER (Product Manager discipline)

Microsoft PM levels run **59–68** on the same numeric scale as engineering, grouped into IC "bands" (IC3 = 61–62, IC4 = 63–64, IC5 = 65–66, etc.). Note Microsoft does NOT publish official PM YOE bands; YOE here is the requester's framing cross-checked against levels.fyi/community sources and is **directional, not official**.

| MS Level | Title (PM discipline) | Band | YOE (directional) | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 59–60 | Product Manager (new grad / entry) | IC2 | 0–2 | levels.fyi shows L59 as floor of PM range ($179K). Some sources tag 59–60 as intern/co-op/graduate-intern — **partially unverified / drifted**; treat 59–60 as entry IC. |
| 61–62 | Product Manager 2 (PM II) | IC3 | 2–9 | **Confirmed** — levels.fyi has live L61 & L62 PM pages. |
| 63–64 | Senior Product Manager | IC4 | 5–18 | **Confirmed** — both L63 & L64 PM pages live. (IC4 band = both "Senior".) |
| 65–66 | Principal Product Manager | IC5 | 10–24 | **Confirmed** — both L65 & L66 PM pages live. **Leadership/Principal scope begins here (~65).** |
| 67 | Partner / Group Product Manager | IC6/M | 17+ | **Confirmed** — live L67 PM page. Title is Partner-band; "Group PM" is a management variant. |
| 68 | Partner Product Manager | IC6+ | 20+ | **Confirmed** — levels.fyi top of public PM ladder ("Partner"). |

**Where leadership begins:** ~L65 (Principal) is where IC scope becomes org-influencing; people-management (Group PM / Partner) typically L67+.
**Flagged drift:** the 59–60 cell — community sources disagree (entry-IC vs intern band). Marked **unverified**.
