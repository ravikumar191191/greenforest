# Cross-Company PM / PM-T Level Equivalence: Amazon/AWS · Microsoft · Google

**Run date:** 2026-06-16
**Requester context:** AWS L6 Senior PM-T (specifically interested in where L6/L7 PMT land at Microsoft and Google, and whether Google down-levels).
**Track scope:** Product Manager / Product Manager – Technical (PM-T / PMT-ES). Engineering (SWE) mappings are cited only where used to triangulate, and are flagged as such.

## 0. Method & source-access notes

- **levels.fyi, Blind, Quora, Fishbowl, Glassdoor, and several Substack pages returned HTTP 403 to direct fetch** during this run. Their content was therefore captured via search-engine indexing/snippets rather than full-page fetch. Treat anecdotal quotes as paraphrased-from-snippet unless marked as a direct quote. URLs are provided for every claim so the requester can open them directly (the pages load fine in a normal browser; they only block automated fetch).
- **Official / semi-official** = levels.fyi's published "compare levels" tool, which derives mappings from crowdsourced comp + overlapping leveling-rubric scope + reported transfers. levels.fyi itself warns it is "an approximation … not everyone at level X at one company will get level Y at another."
- **Anecdotal** = Blind / Reddit / Quora / Fishbowl / Glassdoor individual posters (self-reported, unverifiable, often a single data point).

## 1. Baseline ladders (what each level *is*)

**Amazon / AWS PM ladder** (source: Blind "product manager leveling", levels.fyi):
- L5 = Product Manager (entry/junior PM; PM-T also exists at L5)
- **L6 = Senior PM / Senior PM-T** (in AWS the variant is "PMT-ES", which is comped higher than org-wide PM-T)
- **L7 = Principal PM / Principal PM-T** (or Sr. Manager, PM)
- L8 = Director, PM
- *No Senior Principal (L8 IC) PM title.* PM-T comp runs ~20% above non-technical PM at the same level.

**Microsoft PM ladder** (source: Blind "Microsoft which level are these roles", 6figr, salarynegotiator):
- 62 = PM 2
- **63–64 = Senior PM**
- **65–66 = Principal PM**
- 67 = Group/Partner PM (Partner = 68 bench)

**Google PM ladder** (source: Blind "Google titles vs levels for PM", resumeadapter Google Levels 2026):
- L4 = Associate/PM
- **L5 = Senior PM**  *(note: "Senior" title usage varies; some call L6 "Senior")*
- **L6 = Senior/Staff PM**
- L7 = Group PM / Principal-equivalent (Google has **no "Principal PM" title**)
- L8 = Director

## 5. Verdict vs. the starting hypotheses

1. **"AWS L6 ≈ MS 63–64 ≈ Google L4–L5"** — **Partly confirmed.** Google side: **L5 modal, L4 documented, L6 disputed** → range **L4–L6**. Microsoft side: ladder & posters support **63–64 (Senior)**, but levels.fyi's PM row says **65 (Principal)** → use **63–65**. The L4–L5 down-level camp is real and well-attested for thin-product-tenure candidates.
2. **"AWS L7 ≈ MS 65–66 ≈ Google L6 Senior PM"** — **Mostly confirmed, shift the MS number up:** evidence points to **MS 66–67** (not 65–66) and **Google L6** (with L7 possible, L5 worst-case). 65 is the bottom edge, not the center.
3. **"Amazon bands wider (one L7 spans Google L5–L7)"** — **Confirmed** as the dominant framing across levels.fyi and anecdotal sources; mechanism (scope vs comp-arbitrage) is itself contested.
